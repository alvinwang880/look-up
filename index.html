For teens · and the adults around them

Teens spend over 8½ hours a day on screens.

That's the daily average for 13–18 year olds, not counting schoolwork. Adults aren't far behind, at nearly 7 hours a day online. Apps are designed to keep everyone scrolling, so much of that time slips away without anyone choosing it. Look Up helps teens and their families take it back, one day at a time.

Sources: Common Sense Census 2021 (ages 13–18, entertainment screen use) · DataReportal, Digital 2024 (internet users aged 16–64).

What too much screen time takes from you

Screens are useful. The problem is the hours that go to endless feeds and autoplay, time we'd rarely choose to spend that way if we stopped to think.

Sleep

Teens need 8–10 hours a night and adults 7 or more. Late-night scrolling is linked to falling asleep later and sleeping worse. (American Academy of Sleep Medicine)

Focus

Notifications break concentration, whether it's homework, revision or work. Getting back on track after each interruption takes time.

People

Time on a phone often comes out of time with friends and family, even at the same dinner table.

How Look Up works

  1. Notice

    Copy your daily total from Screen Time or Digital Wellbeing. It takes ten seconds.

  2. Choose

    Pick a daily target that feels achievable, and a reward you'll give yourself.

  3. Put the phone down

    A small daily challenge, a phone-down timer and a wind-down reminder help you do it.

  4. See what you got back

    Every minute under your target is time reclaimed, and it adds up.

For parents and families

Look Up is built for teens, and it works just as well for adults. It works best when the whole family does it together.

Do it together

Everyone uses Look Up on their own phone with their own target. Compare time reclaimed, not screen time, and try a phone-free dinner as a family.

Not a monitoring tool

Nobody can see anyone else's log. A teen's data stays on their own phone, so Look Up stays theirs.

Safe for under-18s

No account, no email, no ads and no tracking. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.
