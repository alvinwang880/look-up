A global habit

The world spends nearly 7 hours a day on screens.

That is the average time a person spends online each day, across every country. Phones and apps are designed to keep us scrolling, and most of that time slips away without us choosing it. Look Up helps you take some of it back, one day at a time.

What too much screen time takes from you

Screens are useful. The problem is the hours that go to endless feeds and autoplay, time we'd rarely choose to spend that way if we stopped to think.

Sleep

Late-night scrolling is linked to falling asleep later and sleeping worse, especially for teenagers.

Focus

Constant notifications break concentration. Getting back into deep work after an interruption takes time.

People

Time on a phone often comes out of time with friends and family, even when we're in the same room.
